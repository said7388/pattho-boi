Delete your Pattho Boi account

Pattho Boi - Textbook Reader (com.sstinnovation.patthoboi) by SST Innovation

অ্যাপ থেকে: প্রোফাইল → নিচে “অ্যাকাউন্ট মুছে ফেলো” চাপো → নিশ্চিত করো। অ্যাকাউন্ট ও সার্ভারে রাখা সব তথ্য সঙ্গে সঙ্গে মুছে যাবে।

অ্যাপ ছাড়া: যে Google অ্যাকাউন্ট দিয়ে লগ ইন করেছিলে, সেই ইমেইল থেকে info.sstinnovation@gmail.com ঠিকানায় “Delete my Pattho Boi account” লিখে ইমেইল করো। ৩০ দিনের মধ্যে মুছে ফেলা হবে।

Option 1: In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Pattho Boi and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll down and tap “অ্যাকাউন্ট মুছে ফেলো” (Delete account), below Log out.
  3. Confirm. You may be asked to choose your Google account again to verify it's you.

Your account and its data are deleted right away.

Option 2: By email (no app needed)

  1. From the Google account you used to sign in, email info.sstinnovation@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject “Delete my Pattho Boi account”.

We will delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: Google name, email, profile photo and account ID
  • Academic profile: class, level, version and department
  • Synced reading progress, bookmarks and daily reading time
  • Feedback messages you sent from the app

What is kept

We keep nothing from a deleted account and hold no backups of it. Reading data and downloaded books saved on your phone are not on our servers. They stay on the device until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

See our Privacy Policy for more details.
