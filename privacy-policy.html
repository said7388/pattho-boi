Privacy Policy

Pattho Boi - Textbook Reader · Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. Who we are

Pattho Boi - Textbook Reader (package name com.sstinnovation.patthoboi) is a free reading app that lets students in Bangladesh browse, download and read the National Curriculum and Textbook Board (NCTB) textbooks on their phone. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

Pattho Boi is an independent app by SST Innovation. It is not a government app, and it is not made by, affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing NCTB, the Ministry of Education, or the Government of Bangladesh. The textbooks themselves are the free e-books published by NCTB and are downloaded from public links (see Section 5).

Original source of the textbooks: nctb.gov.bd (NCTB official website), nctb.gov.bd/pages/publications (NCTB textbooks) and drive.egovcloud.gov.bd (Bangladesh e-Gov Cloud, original book files).

2. Using the app without an account

You can use Pattho Boi as a guest without signing in. In guest mode, everything you set up stays on your device only and is never sent to us:

  • your chosen class, level, version (Bangla/English) and department;
  • reading progress, bookmarks and daily reading time;
  • reader settings such as theme, brightness and font preferences;
  • textbook PDF files you download for offline reading.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes this data.

3. Information we collect when you sign in

Signing in with Google is optional. It lets you back up your progress and continue on another device. If you sign in, we collect:

Data Source Why it is used
Name, email address, profile photo and a unique account ID Your Google account, via Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication To create your account, sign you in and show your profile in the app
Academic profile: class, level, version (Bangla/English), department What you select in the app To show the right textbooks for you
Reading activity: last page read in each book, bookmarks, last book read, and seconds read per day Generated while you use the app To sync your progress across devices and show your reading stats
Feedback messages, with your account ID, email, platform (e.g. Android) and app version Only when you send feedback from Help & Feedback To read and respond to your feedback and fix problems

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, phone number, payment information, or advertising ID. The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

4. Where your data is stored

Signed-in account data is stored in Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google LLC. Security rules ensure each user can read and write only their own data. Feedback can be submitted by signed-in users but cannot be read back through the app. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by Google.

Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security pages.

5. Downloading textbooks

When you open or download a textbook, the app fetches the PDF directly from the public link where NCTB has published it on nctb.gov.bd: the Bangladesh government cloud (drive.egovcloud.gov.bd) or Google Drive. Like any download, those servers receive standard technical information such as your IP address. We do not receive or store this information. Downloaded books are saved in the app's own storage on your device, and you can delete them at any time from the Storage screen.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state – to download textbooks, sign in and sync your progress.
  • Storage – to save downloaded textbooks for offline reading.
  • Notifications (asked only if you turn on the reminder) – to show a daily reading reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your device; no notification data is sent to us.

The app may also adjust screen brightness and keep the screen awake while you read. These settings stay on your device.

7. How we share data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with Google (Firebase and Google Sign-In) as described above, which processes it on our behalf to run the app, or when required by law.

8. How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account is active. Feedback messages are kept as long as needed to handle them. Deleting your account in the app removes it and its synced data immediately. Deletion requests sent by email are completed within 30 days. We do not keep backups of deleted accounts.

9. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Use the app as a guest. No data leaves your device.
  • Sign out at any time from your Profile.
  • Turn off reminders in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.
  • Delete downloaded books from the Storage screen.
  • Delete your account and data in the app. Open Profile and tap “অ্যাকাউন্ট মুছে ফেলো” (Delete account), then confirm. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, academic profile, reading progress, bookmarks and the feedback you sent. Reading data you created on this phone stays on the device until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • Delete your account without the app. Follow the steps on our account deletion page, or email info.sstinnovation@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject “Delete my Pattho Boi account”. We will delete the same data within 30 days and confirm by email.

You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, by emailing the same address.

10. Children's privacy

Pattho Boi is designed for students, including children. The app has no ads, no tracking and no public profiles or chat, and works fully without an account. Signing in is optional and uses only the data listed in Section 3. If you are under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), please ask a parent or guardian before signing in. If a parent or guardian believes their child has given us personal information and wants it removed, they can contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use Google's managed infrastructure, encrypted connections and access rules that limit each account to its own data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. We will post the new version on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Important changes will also be announced in the app or on its Google Play listing.
